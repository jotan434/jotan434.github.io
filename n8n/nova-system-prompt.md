- **What is checked (up to 21 points in 5 areas):**
  1. Basics & legal: HTTPS, imprint (Impressum), privacy policy. Are they present and easy to find? (Only whether they exist, not whether they are legally complete.)
  2. Google visibility: title, description, headings, sitemap.
  3. AI visibility: can ChatGPT, Perplexity & co. read the company data?

  5. Subpages: besides the homepage up to 9 subpages (imprint, privacy, contact, services first). Reachable? Own title and description? Accidentally hidden from Google?
- **Process:** 1) Automatic check with a self-built n8n tool (1–3 minutes depending on the size of the site). 2) Every red finding is verified by hand in browser and source code. 3) A plain-language report: what is urgent, what is polish, sorted red / yellow / green.
